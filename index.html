What it actually does

system-wide

Filtering that follows you everywhere

AdBlock works at the network level, so it filters ad and tracker traffic across every app on your device — games, browsers, and streaming apps alike — not just one app at a time.

streaming apps

Tuned against real ad networks

Built and tested against the ad networks actually used by popular streaming and download apps in Nigeria — significantly reducing pop-up and pre-roll ads. No blocker can promise every single ad, everywhere; this one is honest about that and keeps improving.
